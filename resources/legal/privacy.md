{company} ("we") runs Skooline, a school management system. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

## What we collect

- **School account details:** school name, address, contact person, email and phone, given when a school registers or is added.
- **School records:** the data a school enters (students, staff, attendance, fees, exams). The school owns this data; we process it only to provide the service.
- **Usage and security data:** sign-in times, IP address and device type, kept to protect accounts.

## How we use it

- To provide, secure and support the service.
- To send account emails such as invoices, reminders and password resets.
- We do **not** sell personal data and do not use school records for advertising.

## Payments

Card payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store full card numbers.

## Keeping data safe

Each school's records are kept separate from every other school. Passwords are stored encrypted (hashed), and staff can turn on two-factor sign-in.

## How long we keep data

School records are kept while the school uses Skooline. After an account is closed, a school can ask for an export, and the data is then deleted.

## Your rights

Parents, students and staff should contact their school first, as the school controls its records. You can also contact us at {email}.
